What this is: the ring is drawn at its true circumference (26,659 m) and the four detector cutaways use each experiment's real published length, diameter, mass and magnet field, scaled consistently against each other and against the ring. The beam-energy, γ-factor, √s, and bunch-spacing figures are computed live from CERN's Run 3 design parameters (6.8 TeV/beam, 13.6 TeV collision energy, 25 ns bunch spacing) using E=γmc². What this isn't: the internal detector geometry (calorimeter towers, tracker layers, muon chambers) is a simplified schematic built from primitives, not an as-built CAD import, and "trigger a collision" draws an illustrative track fan-out rather than a GEANT4-level shower simulation.
